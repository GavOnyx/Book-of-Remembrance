## Intro

Welcome to the Book of Remembrance app (aka Remembrance). This was designed with the Area Book / Preach My Gospel app in mind as developed by the LDS church (contacts are integrated with events, and events can be marked as 'happened', etc). But this is not just a calendar app — it is a way to record and plan your life.

## The Home View

- A month overview shows how much of your day is scheduled/planned for.
- A donut chart shows how (or with whom) you spend your time.
- Any events with an unmarked status from the recent past are shown in a list.

## The Calendar View

- Tap on a blank space to add an event, or press the plus button.
- Tap on an event to view and/or edit the details.
- Events have titles, types, start/end times, statuses, locations, descriptions, and associated contacts. This allows for keeping a very detailed record of your life — including who you spend your time with.
- Press and hold an event to multi-select and/or bulk edit selected events.
- Press the Journal icon at the top left to access the Journal View

## The Journal View

- In the Journal view, a greater emphasis is placed on the details of events. Everything is saved as you type and update events. Use the Calendar view to plan your day and the Journal view to flush out the details.
- The large colored dot next to each event title is a drop down to select the Status (unmarked, happened, or didn't happen).  Tap an event title to edit it. The drop down to the right of the title is to select an event type. The start and end times are also selectable tor editing. And tap on the 'add contacts' text or the 'with [contact name]' to update the associated contacts.
- Press the calendar button in the top left to access the calendar view.

## The Contacts View

- Import your phone's contacts via the 'sync' button on the top bar.
- Tap any contact to view its details.
- Press and hold a contact to multi-select to merge or bulk edit.
- Set a contact's relationship while editing for clear sorting and management.
- A list of all the events associated with a contact is shown below the contact's details.

## Settings

- Change the app appearance.
- Manage event and relationship types.
- Change calendar start and end times.
- Import/export data.

## Specific settings info

**Day fill Window**: This setting affects what time-frame the month overview counts as the day. For example, you may not want to worry about what you fill your time with before 4 AM, or after 11 PM because it's almost always sleep. Or maybe most of your life is already planned for and all you want to worry about is how you've used your time in the afternoon.

**Export events**: This exports the events in a machine-readable format (an ics file). It will also export all contacts associated with any of the events by default.

**Export journal**: This exports the events in a human-readable format meant to imitate a journal. Only events with a description/report are included. This is to encourage accurate and detailed record keeping.

**Appearance:** Select from a default theme --the Light and Dark themes are based on the default PMG themes. Make a custom theme by editing an existing theme and then ***pressing the save button*** which appears near the theme drop down selector. Whatever the theme colors are currently set to will become the new Custom theme after pressing the save button.  

**Importing Events/Contacts**: Be sure to import contacts before importing events. Events with contacts that aren't in your list will lose those links unless the auto-create toggle is on.

**Device Sharing**: share events and contacts with another Remembrance user either as a single transfer, or remember them as a sync partner — useful for spouses and companions alike.

## Privacy

Everything stays on your device. There is no account, no cloud, and no tracking. Network permissions only allow the app to send/receive data from other Book of Remembrance users, and only if you explicitly tell it to.
